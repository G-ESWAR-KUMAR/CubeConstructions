Small Business Outreach

Cube Constructions is dedicated to empowering and supporting the growth of local, small, and disadvantaged businesses in the construction industry. We have established strong partnerships with many small businesses, enabling them to thrive in today's competitive environment. Over the past five years, we have proudly awarded a significant portion of our subcontract work to SB, SDB, WOSB, HUBZone, VOSB, and SDVOSB firms on various projects.

Our outreach initiatives and involvement in small business organizations allow us to engage with subcontractors and suppliers at both local and national levels. As part of our mission, we are committed to promoting, advocating, and utilizing all small business enterprises.

How To Apply

Join our growing network of small business partners by completing the Subcontractor Prequalification Form. This helps us evaluate your company's capabilities and connect you with suitable future projects. You can submit the completed form via mail, fax, or email.
