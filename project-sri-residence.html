Project Overview

Sri Residence is a premium residential apartment development located in a peaceful and well-connected locality. Constructed on a 300 sq. yd. site with a total built-up area of approximately 10,200 sq. ft., this project features spacious 3 BHK apartments designed for comfortable family living.

Each apartment at Sri Residence is thoughtfully planned with modern finishes, efficient layouts, and abundant natural light. The project reflects Cube Constructions' commitment to quality construction, efficient space planning, and long-term value creation in a prime Vijayawada location.

With its strategic location, premium specifications, and meticulous attention to detail, Sri Residence offers residents a lifestyle of comfort, convenience, and elegance — making it a truly desirable address.
